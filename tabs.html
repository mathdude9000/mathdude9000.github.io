Here are some great resources to get tabs

Mr.Tabs

This youtube channel has lots of songs with tabs so you can learn songs at your own pace. The channel has a lot of popular songs by famous artists. I like the channel because you can see him playing it as the tabs go.


Ultimate guitar

This website has tabs for basicily any song created. You can look up songs and can see the chord and tab versions of songs.
