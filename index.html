Good brands

Fender

Fender guitars work very well and their very affordable, but if you are a serious guitarist it's not the best choice for a guitar. There are a lot much higher quality brans that sound and look better. Overall Fender is a great brand of guitar for Beginner to intermediate players.Prices start at $200 and go to around $2000 (It depends if there is certian limited edtion guitars out)

LTD

Overall LTD has products that are usualy a little higher quality then Fender but they are a little more expensive.They work very well for metal and rock.There is still much higher quality brands that sound, look and are bulit better. But I recomend LTD as a brand for intermediate guitar players because of it's great playablity for metal and rock, it's great sound, and it's overall affordability.

ESP

Esp is a very high qulaity brand bulit for serious musicians or people who play professionally. They swound amazing and have so many different cool designs to pick from. With great sound and cool looks equals more money. The cheapest new ESP is around $4900 (Almost $3000 more than the most expensive LTD)the most expensive is about $8300. These are great guitars that will not disipoint but it will disipoint your wallet. Overall ESP is a great brand for serious musicians who want top tier guitars for peak preformance, sound, looks, and playability
